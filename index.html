About

Mattias Milger - Projects

A landing page for my projects. Hosted apps open straight in your browser. The rest are on GitHub to download and run yourself. Runs entirely in your browser with no server.

Features

Browsing

  • Live - hosted on GitHub Pages or external services. Click Open app to use it. Marked with a coloured edge.
  • Downloadable - not hosted. Click GitHub to download it. Marked with a grey edge.
  • Availability filter - show all projects, only live ones, or only downloadable ones.

Arranging sections

  • Move a section - click and drag anywhere on it, except on the buttons. On a touch screen, press and hold for a moment, then drag.
  • Reset order - the small button at the bottom restores the default order.

Theme

  • Toggle Theme - switch between dark (default) and light.

Privacy

Nothing is sent anywhere. Only your theme choice and section order are saved in this browser.
